## 1. Evidence escalation

**Source ref:** knowledge/contracts/policy-analyst-v1/cpa1-f030-evidence-policy.md#1-evidence-escalation

This procedure governs biologics capacity evidence review for CPA1-F030 only. Conflicting sponsor and provider acceptance statuses for the same billed service item trigger evidence escalation; neither status is assumed to override the other. Reconciliation of the statuses is necessary before a billability conclusion. Unverified prerequisites require evidence review rather than assumed approval. Wrong-item records do not confirm the billed item. A signed exception is effective only when the SOW expressly permits it and CPA1-F030 sponsor commercial officer signs it before service, identifying the billed service ID. The controller retains all payment and business-system decisions.
