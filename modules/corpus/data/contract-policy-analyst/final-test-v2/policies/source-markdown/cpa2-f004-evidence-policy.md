## 1. Evidence escalation

**Source ref:** knowledge/contracts/policy-analyst-v2/cpa2-f004-evidence-policy.md#1-evidence-escalation

This fictional procedure applies only to supplier CPA2-F004 in January 2027. Conflicting sponsor and provider quality statuses for the same billed service item require evidence escalation and reconciliation before billability is resolved. Neither party's status automatically prevails. Unverified prerequisites require evidence review. A record identifying another supplier, invoice line or service ID cannot confirm the billed item. The controller retains all payment and business-system decisions; evidence direction is not an executed action.
